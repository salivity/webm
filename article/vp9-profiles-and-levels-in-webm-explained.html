VP9 Profiles and Levels in WebM Explained

VP9 video streams encapsulated within the WebM container standard rely on defined profiles and levels to ensure decoder capability and smooth playback across different hardware and software environments. This guide breaks down the four distinct VP9 profiles, details the standard operating levels from 1 to 6.2, and explains how these parameters are signaled inside the WebM container.

VP9 Profiles

VP9 defines four profiles (0 through 3), which specify the color depth, chroma subsampling, and color space capabilities:

  • Profile 0: Standard 8-bit color depth with 4:2:0 chroma subsampling. This is the most widely supported profile across web browsers and legacy hardware decoders.
  • Profile 1: Standard 8-bit color depth supporting advanced chroma subsampling, including 4:2:2 and 4:4:4, alongside full-color channel support.
  • Profile 2: High bit-depth profile supporting 10-bit and 12-bit color depth with 4:2:0 chroma subsampling. This profile is standard for High Dynamic Range (HDR10 and HLG) video delivery.
  • Profile 3: Premium high bit-depth profile supporting 10-bit and 12-bit color with 4:2:2 or 4:4:4 chroma subsampling.

VP9 Levels

Levels define the decoder performance limits, including maximum resolution, frame rate, sample rate, and bitrate constraints. VP9 defines 14 official levels:

  • Level 1 (1.0): 256×144 @ 15 fps (Max bitrate: 200 kbps)
  • Level 1.1: 384×288 @ 15 fps (Max bitrate: 800 kbps)
  • Level 2 (2.0): 480×360 @ 30 fps (Max bitrate: 1.8 Mbps)
  • Level 2.1: 640×480 @ 30 fps (Max bitrate: 3.6 Mbps)
  • Level 3 (3.0): 1080×720 @ 30 fps (Max bitrate: 7.2 Mbps)
  • Level 3.1: 1280×720 @ 30 fps (Max bitrate: 12 Mbps)
  • Level 4 (4.0): 1920×1080 @ 30 fps (Max bitrate: 18 Mbps)
  • Level 4.1: 1920×1080 @ 60 fps (Max bitrate: 30 Mbps)
  • Level 5 (5.0): 3840×2160 (4K) @ 30 fps (Max bitrate: 60 Mbps)
  • Level 5.1: 3840×2160 (4K) @ 60 fps (Max bitrate: 90 Mbps)
  • Level 5.2: 3840×2160 (4K) @ 120 fps (Max bitrate: 180 Mbps)
  • Level 6 (6.0): 7680×4320 (8K) @ 30 fps (Max bitrate: 180 Mbps)
  • Level 6.1: 7680×4320 (8K) @ 60 fps (Max bitrate: 240 Mbps)
  • Level 6.2: 7680×4320 (8K) @ 120 fps (Max bitrate: 480 Mbps)

WebM Container Encapsulation

When a VP9 stream is packaged in a WebM (Matroska-based) container:

  • Codec ID: The stream is identified with the Matroska element CodecID set to V_VP9.
  • CodecPrivate Metadata: The specific profile, level, bit depth, and chroma subsampling are signaled inside the WebM track header using the CodecPrivate element (specifically formatted as VP9 Codec Feature Metadata). This allows media players and browser media engines (via MSE/EME) to parse playback requirements before initiating hardware decoding.
